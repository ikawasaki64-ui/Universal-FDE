# Repository guidance

For a user request to train or improve a real business workflow with Universal FDE, use `.codex/skills/universal-fde/SKILL.md` as the entrypoint. Follow the two authoritative project documents and their interaction/runtime rules; do not invent or skip stages. The skill controls which parts of the training protocol may be read at each point.

Do not activate Universal FDE for unrelated repository questions. When a new workflow-training project starts, introduce the method briefly and begin its Stage 00 interview with the minimum critical questions. For an existing project, resume from its persisted `FDE_PROJECT_STATE` and current Stage.
